♟️

Chess-Native Structure

Define your own levels — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or whatever your academy uses. Online/offline modes and flexible session schedules, built the way coaches actually work.

✅

One-Tap Attendance

Coaches mark PRESENT / ABSENT in under 60 seconds per batch. Timestamped, logged, and accessible to the admin.

💰

Flexible Billing Engine

Per session or by the hour (recurring sessions) — per batch, per student. Apply discount codes and custom fee overrides. Fees auto-debit from each student's prepaid wallet. No more manual calculations.

👩‍💻

Student & Parent Portals

Students and parents log in to see schedules, attendance history, session statements, and wallet summary. Reduces routine follow-up queries to the coach.

📊

Powerful Reports

Attendance per student, revenue per batch, sessions per coach, outstanding payments. Pull any report instantly or configure automated scheduled email delivery.

🔒

Roles & Permissions

Admin, Manager, Coach, Student, and Parent — each role sees only what it should. Full audit log of every action.

💳

Online Payment Collection

On Professional and Enterprise, students top up wallets via Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal, or Mollie — depending on your country. Admin enables the gateway from the dashboard. Top-ups cover session fees automatically.

Bulk Student Import

Import hundreds of students at once via a CSV file. The system validates every row — catching errors before committing — and reports them clearly. Enterprise plan feature.

🎥

Zoom Classroom

Connect your Zoom account. Coaches start live class from the session; students and parents join from their portal. Included on every plan.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to run this software? ▾
A Linux VPS with at least 2 vCPU and 4 GB RAM, Docker, and a domain — or choose Axeron-hosted and we run a private instance for you. If you self-host and don't have a server yet, we'll help you set it up. Hosting is quoted with your license.
Do I need a technical background to manage this? ▾
No. Once it's installed, day-to-day use is a web dashboard. We do the initial install — on your VPS or on the private instance we host — during onboarding.
Is this a SaaS I can register for and start using today? ▾
No. There is no public “create academy” signup. ChessDesk is licensed per academy and provisioned as a dedicated install. We can host that install for you, but it is still isolated from other academies — not a shared multi-tenant site.
What happens when my license expires? ▾
Access is suspended when the license period ends. On a self-hosted install, your data remains on your server. On an Axeron-hosted instance, we retain your data according to the license terms so you can renew and resume. We'll notify you before expiry.
Can I import my existing student data in bulk? ▾
Yes. On the Enterprise plan, bulk CSV import is included. Download the template CSV, fill in your student data, and upload it — the system validates every row and shows any errors before committing. For Starter and Professional, students are added manually (2–3 minutes each) or we can quote a one-time migration.
Can students pay fees online? ▾
Yes. On Professional and Enterprise, the admin enables a payment gateway from Payment Settings — Razorpay (India), Stripe, PayPal, or Mollie (EU), depending on your country. Students and parents can then top up wallets from their portal. Once topped up, session fees are auto-debited from the wallet balance.
Can I upgrade from Starter to Professional later? ▾
Yes. Contact us and we'll calculate the upgrade cost for the remaining period of your license. Your data stays intact — it's the same software, just with the higher tier unlocked.
Is there a free trial? ▾
We offer a live demo tailored to your academy's structure instead of a self-serve trial — this is more useful than clicking around an unfamiliar demo environment. Book a 15-minute call and we'll show you exactly how it works for your batches and student count. Alternatively, we can set up a 14-day trial instance on request.
